# 2021 Madoi Earthquake Test Data
This directory contains high-rate GNSS displacement waveforms for testing FRONT and PI-FRONT on the 2021 Mw 7.4 Madoi (Maduo) earthquake. The source study by [Chen et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021GL095243) used **12** high-rate GNSS stations.
## Data source and citation
Chen, K., Avouac, J.-P., Geng, J., Liang, C., Zhang, Z., Li, Z., & Zhang, S. (2022). The 2021 Mw 7.4 Madoi earthquake: An archetype bilateral slip-pulse rupture arrested at a splay fault. *Geophysical Research Letters, 49*(2), e2021GL095243. [https://doi.org/10.1029/2021GL095243](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021GL095243).

The paper states that its high-rate GNSS waveforms are available as SAC files in the article's supplementary dataset. Cite the paper and its supplementary dataset when reusing these observations.
